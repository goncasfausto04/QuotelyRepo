Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Quotely helps users request and compare quotes from suppliers. This page explains what data Quotely handles.

Data we collect

  • Account information (email address, name, optional profile photo).
  • Briefings, conversations and quotes you create in the app.
  • Emails exchanged with suppliers about your briefings.

Google user data

Quotely uses the Gmail API only on its own sending account to send quote requests to suppliers and read their replies to those requests. Gmail data is used solely to show supplier replies inside Quotely. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties except as needed to provide the service (e.g. AI analysis of a supplier's quote). Quotely's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored with our hosting providers (Supabase, Render, Vercel). You can delete your briefings in the app, or contact us to delete your account and all associated data.
